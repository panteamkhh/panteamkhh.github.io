About me

I'm a data analyst and machine-learning enthusiast who likes the unglamorous parts as much as the modelling: cleaning, validation, tests and reproducibility. Most of my projects come with a documented pipeline, a pytest suite and charts I'd be happy to defend.

I work mostly in Python (pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib) and Power BI, and I've been building with LLMs and agents lately. I care about honest metrics — macro-F1 over a flattering accuracy score, loss ratios over vanity dashboards.
